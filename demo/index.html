Fine-tuned: YOLOv8n fine-tuned for 10 epochs on the Roboflow “car-obsticles” dataset (CC BY 4.0); val mAP@0.5 0.674 on that dataset's validation split. It generalises poorly to new street scenes (see the README). COCO baseline: stock Ultralytics YOLOv8n. Both are Ultralytics models, AGPL-3.0. Runtime: onnxruntime-web 1.20.1 (WASM, CPU).
